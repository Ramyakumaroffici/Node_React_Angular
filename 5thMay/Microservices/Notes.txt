Microservices-

It is the collection of small independent services and each
service is responsible for specific business functionality.

instead of creating one large application, with the help 
of Microservices we can break it into smaller pieces

eg-
payment, order, notification, user service 

** each Microservice runs independently and it has its own 
database(optional) , they can communicate with each other 
via API's 

all services will work together but they are loosely coupled.

if one service got crashed it will not affect anyother services.

--------------------------------------------------------

Features of Microservices-

1) each service can be deployed without affecting the other 
service.

2) All services are not tightly dependent on each other.

3) we can scale the service as per the requirement of the service .  
eg - Payment during peak sales.

4) If one service fails other services will continue 
working.

--------------------------------------------------------

Architecture of Microservices-

It is based on the design pattern that has structure 
(collection of small independent services).

It consist of 

1) service component-

service that consist of specific business functionality.

2) Api gateway - 

it is the single entry point for the client request.

3) database - it service has its own database to maintain 
loose coupling.

4) service can communicate via protocols.

5) security - authentication

6) CI/CD - Automates building of the application, testing ,deployment
eg - jenkins
